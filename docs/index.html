Mean DTI over four strand-blocked folds.

Whole mapped strands are deleted from the catalogue inside one spatial block, the retained catalogue is masked out of every term of the official index, and the model is asked to find the deleted strands from data alone. Every strategy sees identical truth, identical emission domain and identical masses.

Valid file. Real holdout skill. Unknown live score.

The H42 candidate passes every format check and shows large, controlled skill on an instrument that asks the prize question. It has never been scored by the organizer, and the holdout's truth is withheld mapped strands while the prize round's truth is faults that were never mapped at all. Those are not the same population, so the holdout DTI is an upper-leaning estimate, not a prediction.

WHY THE MASS IS 20,000 · NEW THIS SESSION

The family's own score record, read as a measurement.

Twenty-nine published rasters with reported scores are probes of the hidden label set. Five of them form an exactly nested lineage (containment 1.0000, verified from the bytes), and for a nested lineage the official index collapses to a straight line: 1/score = 2.9884 + 1.7871e-05 × mass, R² = 0.9922 over the eight-point chain. The intercept gives the incumbent belief field's coverage a = 0.2869 and its ceiling 0.3346 — above the 0.3262 that headed the dated public snapshot — while the slope gives a hidden label count ρ ≈ 36,467, 2.8× the constant this repository previously carried.

The null model is verified against a live score: the family's arrangement-matched scatter control (same mass, same separation, same catalogue-distance distribution, randomised positions) was reported at 0.0778; a position-blind label density puts its ceiling at 0.0776, a relative error of +0.22%.

TWO GEOMETRIC CRITERIA

From fault traces to a falsifiable prediction.

01

Separate the populations

Assign axial strike to NW or N/NNE geometry. Retain every classification, including ambiguous and source-slip-discordant traces.

02

Connect genuine endpoints

Find the nearest N/NNE receiver within 5 km of an outward-facing NW endpoint. Reject connected fragments and boundary tips.

03

Check local orientation

Compare a topographic tangent proxy with the geometrically closer population. No new magnetic, gravity, thermal, or conductivity transform.
