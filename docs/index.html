Evidence, not projections

What is—and is not—validated

The public competition target is expert-mapped faults absent from the existing catalogue. A known-fault holdout is only a proxy for hidden-label discovery.

H41-A · current candidate

Inter-population corridors

39,793 nonzero cells carry 30,000 expected mass, but the requested 90,000-cell soft support was unavailable. Every emitted cell is outside 300 m of the known catalogue, within 500 m of a paired-corridor centerline, and within 2 km of both mapped strike families. This is a geometrical distribution check, not validation of fault truth.

Holdout · H41-A-PRE-1

Unable to generalize across large blocks

In all four quadrants, the NW-tip-to-normal corridor field had zero eligible cells after hiding the block and a 3.3 km collar. The terrain-only baseline scored 0.04689 pooled at matched mass; there was no candidate score to compare against it.

Uniqueness · support masks

Low overlap with retrievable priors

Only 423 support cells overlap each of the H27-4 base and H33-2-B2 rasters. Jaccard is 0.00507 vs H27-4 and 0.00549 vs H33-2-B2. This verifies low support-mask overlap with two available artifacts—not uniqueness against every prior submission.
