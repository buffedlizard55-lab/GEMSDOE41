SECOND RESEARCH CANDIDATE · CATALOGUE-COMPLETION/H43

Learn what a fault looks like, then look where none is mapped.

An independent third lineage: a gradient-boosted classifier over all 19 official competition bands (18 never used in this repository before) plus USGS 3DEP 1 m scarp morphology, trained to reconstruct withheld mapped faults and applied only where no fault is mapped. Emission is maximum-coverage selection over the 300 m kernel, because TP_w is a maximum per truth pixel: a second dot inside an already-covered kernel earns nothing and still pays 0.2 of its mass.

On four strand-blocked folds it scores 0.5606 mean DTI at 20,000 px against a position-blind null of 0.0323 — a factor of 17.3×, in 4/4 folds at every mass tested.

H42 / THE SHIPPED SURFACE

From geophysics to a falsifiable prediction.

01

Terrain relief at basin margins

Slope × (1 − detrended elevation), i.e. steep terrain at low relief position: the range-front setting where Great Basin geothermal systems sit. (slope^0.5 · (1−det)^1.5)^0.55

02

1 m LiDAR scarp corroboration

USGS 3DEP scarp evidence (public, no use restrictions) enters as a 0.25 exponent, so a candidate must show surface-rupture evidence as well as favourable relief position.

03

Magnetic edges and packing

Reduced-to-pole magnetics enter at 0.20. Greedy packing enforces 400 m minimum spacing to 40,000 px, then everything within 200 m of the catalogue is deleted.

p(x) = ( slope^0.5 · (1 − det_elev)^1.5 )^0.55 · scarp^0.25 · tmi_hg^0.20
emit: greedy top-40,000 px at ≥4 px separation, inside footprint, >2 px from any catalogue pixel

Surface, spacing and mass were fixed by a declared rule registered before the final build: highest blocked-holdout mean DTI at equal mass, cross-checked against the hidden-truth association measured from the 16 archived artifacts.

ARCHIVED / NOT THE SHIPPED FILE

The earlier structural-transfer field.

01

Separate the populations

Assign axial strike to NW or N/NNE geometry. Retain every classification, including ambiguous and source-slip-discordant traces.

02

Connect genuine endpoints

Find the nearest N/NNE receiver within 5 km of an outward-facing NW endpoint. Reject connected fragments and boundary tips.

03

Check local orientation

Compare a topographic tangent proxy with the geometrically closer population. No new magnetic, gravity, thermal, or conductivity transform.
