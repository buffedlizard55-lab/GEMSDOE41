What is actually being claimed here

Three separate things are true at once, and this site keeps them apart:

  1. A measured, positive result. On a strand-level held-out split of the published catalogue, a kinematic ranking of the incumbent support beats an uninformative control of the same support at every one of six guard/separation settings. That is the one number here that is an instrument-internal comparison.
  2. A measured, negative result. The hypothesis that transfer corridors are where the hidden faults are was not supported by three recorded diagnostics, including one that does not use the catalogue at all. It is reported on the Irregularities page rather than buried.
  3. A model. Projected scores depend on a constant inferred from a hidden set. They are tagged MODEL everywhere and are never called measurements.

Start with the executive summary for the plain-language version, validation for the experiment, and submit for the file.
