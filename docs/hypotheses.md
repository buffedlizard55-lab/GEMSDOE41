# Preregistered candidate hypotheses (2026-10-05)

The candidate shortlist and operational thresholds below were written **before the first registered H41-A holdout run**. They record the project charter's test plan; the observed holdout result is appended after the preregistration. Expected improvement is a relative scientific prior, not a score forecast. No submission slot is authorized by this document.

## Source-grounded problem framing

The competition asks for newly identified fault structures and uses a distance-weighted Tversky score. A locally observed public leaderboard score is not evidence that a particular method or raster earned it. See [`PROJECT_BRIEF.md`](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) for the dated leaderboard read, H33 score-status discrepancy, input provenance, and the correction to the Emerson Pass/Astor Pass paper attribution.

The structural premise is supported at two different scales and localities:

- Faulds, Henry & Hinz (2005) describe the northern Walker Lane as a system of NW-striking dextral faults that terminate into roughly N-striking normal faults: [DOI 10.1130/G21274.1](https://doi.org/10.1130/G21274.1).
- Anderson & Faulds' Emerson Pass study (2012) describes geothermal permeability around intersecting, terminating normal faults and a north-to-northwest-striking fault, and records the local WNW extension direction: [DOE OSTI 1110518](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1110518).
- Siler, Mayhew & Faulds (2012) is an analogous but distinct blind system at **Astor Pass**, not Emerson Pass: [DOE OSTI 1110516](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1110516).

These studies justify testing a structural arrangement. They do **not** establish that every rasterized fault trace in the competition catalogue has known slip sense, nor that every NW or N-striking intersection is geothermal. Fault strike is inferred from a 100 m raster in this implementation, so it is an approximation to original vector geometry.

## Novelty audit performed before H41-A implementation

Inspected evidence is limited to the supplied history and retrievable linked pages:

- GEMSDOE32's linked candidate reports H33-2-B2 as a 200 m catalogue-flank deletion of an existing emission, not a new cross-family corridor detector. The page explicitly labels that file unscored.
- GEMSDOE32's hypotheses page describes H33-5 as extension of a detector ridge field at tips/stepovers; that is related in broad motivation, but not the paired NW-tip-to-nearest-N-normal-line operator preregistered here.
- GEMSDOE39's linked H39X-01 manifest describes geodetic second-invariant/shear and dilatation transforms, not fault-family endpoint geometry. Its exposed proxy run was exploratory and its formula was recorded after scoring; we will not recycle it.
- GEMSDOE39's H40-E page reports a multi-band out-of-fold discriminant with an SGMC prior. It is a different information source and objective.
- The current GEMSDOE41 checkout was initially empty apart from the title README.

**Scope of uniqueness:** H41-A is a newly implemented method in this checkout and is not copied from a downloaded submission. We do not claim a proof of uniqueness against every historical repo or every unavailable GEMSDOE40–44 artifact. We will compare output rasters against accessible public prior artifacts and report overlap; a human review of any inaccessible artifact remains outside this sandbox's evidence.

## Numerical parameterization (locked before the first holdout run)

These operational thresholds are modeling choices, not values asserted by the papers:

- Estimate axial local strike from a skeletonized 100 m label raster with a 1.0 px smoothing sigma and 2.5 px structure-tensor window; require coherence ≥0.25.
- Classify the target families using non-overlapping axial strikes: NW = 120°–150° (bearings 300°–330°) and N-to-NNE = 0°–30°. Leave all other or low-coherence skeleton cells unclassified; do not infer dextral/normal kinematics from the raster itself.
- Define an endpoint as a skeleton cell with exactly one 8-neighbour skeleton neighbour; retain it as an NW tip only when it is within 4 px of an NW-family trace. Pair each eligible NW endpoint with its nearest N/NNE trace only when the separation is ≤30 px (3 km). Rasterize the straight tip-to-trace centerline; corridor sigma = 3 px and support limit = 5 px.
- Use `det_elev` alone for a topographic orientation/coherence proxy: masked Gaussian smoothing sigma 1.2 px; tensor sigma 2.0 px; retain gradient magnitude relative to the 70th percentile and score axial orientation agreement with tolerance 20°.
- Require both fault families within 20 px (2 km), family-distance imbalance ≤12 px (1.2 km), and exclude any emitted probability within 3 px (300 m) of **any** known fault trace. The 300 m exclusion is an intentional novelty filter, not a claim that new-fault truth cannot lie near known traces.
- Normalize each score field to [0,1]; for matched-mass DTI comparisons, retain the top three times the expected mass in cells and water-fill soft probabilities to exactly 7,500 expected mass per held-out fold (30,000 over four folds).
- Use a 33 px inward collar (3.3 km) for holdout evaluation and also remove that collar from the training fault raster before deriving tips, avoiding artificial endpoints at fold boundaries.

### Evaluation question

Does the full H41-A score field improve the official DTI proxy over the best of (a) a terrain-lineament-only field and (b) a structural-corridor-without-terrain-confirmation field when each arm is emitted at the same per-fold probability mass and scored in spatially held-out regions?

### Split and controls

- Use four deterministic spatial blocks (NW, NE, SW, SE) on the competition grid. For each fold, hide all label pixels in that block from trace extraction/classification and score only in that block, with a preregistered inward collar to reduce boundary leakage.
- Keep the topographic input available in the held-out region because it is a predictor, not a label. Do not use held-out fault pixels to find endpoints, estimate strike populations, pair traces, or tune thresholds.
- Use the official competition distance-weighted Tversky formula (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, R 300 m) and a **matched emission budget** for all arms. Report each fold and pooled metric; no equal-mass comparison is omitted.
- Compare against the best baseline arm within the exact same split and protocol. The historical H40-E/H39X reports are owner-maintained, use different masks/data/protocols, and are not a directly comparable benchmark.
- Report how many emitted pixels lie within 100/200/300 m of each family, how many lie in a geometrically defined pair corridor, and the nearest-trace-distance distribution. The discovery condition fails if the file largely reproduces either known-family density.

### Promotion rule

A file may be called **slot-eligible** only if (i) the candidate improves the best matched baseline on at least 3/4 spatial folds, (ii) the gain survives a collar / orientation-window sensitivity check, (iii) the uniqueness/format audit passes, and (iv) no data/provenance issue invalidates the test. A catalogue holdout win is only a screening result, not evidence of a leaderboard score or hidden-fault discovery. If this gate fails, keep any GeoTIFF as an **unscored research candidate** and do not use a weekly slot.

**Protocol limitation:** H41-A-PRE-1 locks the primary field thresholds and four-quadrant test, but did not numerically define sensitivity variants before the first holdout. No sensitivity result is claimed. Because the primary candidate had no support in any held-out interior, the experiment stopped at that failure; any future sensitivity analysis requires a new dated protocol with its variants frozen before scoring.

## Observed registered result (2026-10-05 UTC)

The primary H41-A-PRE-1 run completed with the preregistered four-quadrant split, 33 px collar, 7,500 expected mass per fold, and support factor 3. The full candidate and corridor-only ablation each had **zero eligible cells in every evaluation interior** (NW, NE, SW, SE). The candidate DTI is **null / unevaluable**, not 0. The terrain-only matched-mass control had fold DTI 0.03032, 0.05950, 0.06695, and 0.05834; pooled DTI = **0.046888**. These control values are not a candidate comparison because H41-A had no matched-mass support. The primary gate failed, no fold comparison was valid, sensitivity was not run, and `slot_eligible=false`.

The full-catalogue offline raster was still emitted, strictly as an unscored research artifact. It has 39,793 nonzero cells carrying 30,000.000 expected probability mass; the requested 90,000-cell support was unavailable, so the support-factor-3 target was not met. All emitted cells are >300 m from the known catalogue; 39,793/39,793 are within 500 m of a registered pair centerline and within 2 km of both families. Median distances are 500 m to any mapped trace, 721 m to the NW family, and 1,000 m to the N/NNE family. This is geometrical concentration by construction, not a validation of fault truth.

The candidate support mask shares 423 pixels with each inspected prior: 44,090 H27-4-base positives (Jaccard 0.00507; 1.063% of candidate support contained) and 37,654 H33-2-B2 positives (Jaccard 0.00549; 1.063% contained). The evidence and file hashes are in `docs/evidence/h41a_build_receipt.json`; the independent format check is `docs/evidence/h41a_format_audit.json`. These two comparisons do not prove uniqueness against inaccessible artifacts.

**Decision:** the preregistered gate did not pass. No slot is recommended. A new CV geometry, collar, or orientation window must be defined in a separate dated preregistration before any additional scoring; it cannot retroactively replace H41-A-PRE-1.
