At a glance

What the raster predicts

39,793 nonzero cells with 30,000 expected probability mass. Only 39,793 eligible cells were available against the 90,000-cell (3× mass) support target, so the preregistered support factor was not met. All emitted cells are more than 300 m from the known catalogue, within 500 m of a registered pair-corridor centerline, and within 2 km of both mapped strike families.

What the holdout found

Four deterministic quadrants; 3.3 km collar; 7,500 matched expected mass per fold. H41-A and corridor-only each had zero eligible pixels in all four evaluation interiors. Terrain-only pooled DTI was 0.046888, but candidate comparison was unavailable.

Format checks

Single-band float32; EPSG:32611; 3730×3292; 100 m; exact template transform and bounds; finite in-footprint predictions in [0,1]; NaN outside the template footprint with a NaN nodata tag.

Novelty check

Support Jaccard is 0.00507 vs the H27-4 base and 0.00549 vs H33-2-B2 (423 overlapping cells in each comparison). This is a measured low overlap with two retrievable prior masks—not a proof of global uniqueness.

Evidence: holdout report · build and uniqueness receipt · independent format audit · input/pixelwise audit · prior-raster overlap audit.

Scientific caveats that matter

300 m novelty filter: cells within 300 m of any mapped trace are deliberately removed to avoid catalogue-density echoes. Competition staff have said valid truth can occur within that distance. The filter may therefore miss legitimate new-fault corrections; it is not a ground-truth boundary.
  • Mapped-fault strike is estimated from a skeletonized 100 m raster, not original vector geometry; the raster carries no trace-level slip-sense attribute.
  • “NW dextral” and “N/NNE normal” are regional structural interpretations, not measured kinematics for each local segment.
  • `det_elev` orientation/coherence is an unsupervised terrain proxy, not a field-mapped fault-lineament layer.
  • The strict quadrant holdout removes the held-out region and a collar from training. That exposes H41-A's lack of long-range generalization, but does not prove it cannot find local off-catalogue structures near visible traces.
  • The input rasters were obtained through a public owner-maintained mirror and match its hashes; they were not independently authenticated against the login-gated DrivenData download.
  • DrivenData's published format says outside-area values should be null/NaN. This raster preserves the local example's NaN footprint convention, but portal validation was not performed because no authenticated upload was made.

H33-2-B2 and the 0.2778 claim

The GEMSDOE32 project page labels its H33-2-B2 file UNSCORED, reports a 0.2747 model projection, and says no artifact in that repository has an organizer score. A later GEMSDOE39 pull request calls 0.2778 “owner-reported” and describes deleting 2,545 more pixels from a 0.2708 base, specifically cells 1.414–2 pixels from the mapped catalogue. Such pruning could raise score if the removed mass loses more hidden-label credit than it saves in false-positive penalty, but that mechanism is an interpretation—not a verified official explanation. The public leaderboard's 0.2778 row is #13 for `extradr19`; it does not link that score to H33-2-B2.

Current official #1 is 0.3262 (`nchuzhoy`, checked 2026-10-05 UTC). No evidence from H41-A shows it can beat either 0.2778 or 0.3262. See the live leaderboard and source ledger.
