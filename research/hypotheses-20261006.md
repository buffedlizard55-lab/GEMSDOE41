# Preregistration — 2026-10-06: structural position, not recycled prediction

Read alongside the complete original prompt in README.md. This registration is written before implementing or scoring the candidates below. Ranking is qualitative scientific judgement about expected DTI utility, not a forecast of leaderboard points. No new magnetic, gravity, thermal, or other geophysical transform is permitted. All candidates use the already obtained, checksum-pinned official GDR 1391 v2 fault vectors and the supplied detrended-elevation band 12. No new external data are required or presumed available.

## Fixed model

Keep H41-A Config unchanged: 135°/10° axial family prototypes, 30° anchor tolerance, 600 m minimum trace, 5 km maximum gap, 500 m corridor sigma, outward cosine ≥0.5 for gaps >200 m, authentic endpoints, 200 m catalogue exclusion, existing coherence detector. Every trace retains its nearest-prototype geometric class; source kinematics are not inferred from strike.

Replace only the orientation reference: for each positive corridor pixel choose the closer family using the original all-trace raster distance fields (ties choose N/NNE, as H41-A). Find the nearest full vector part of that family by exact GEOS distance; at its closest point, estimate axial tangent using points ±250 m along the trace, clipped to its authentic endpoints. A zero-length local chord receives zero confidence. The score is `clip(corridor * cos(axial_difference(local_lineament,local_trace_tangent))**8 * detected, 0, 1)`. Nearest-vector ties are broken by retained source order. Fixed bounded normalization, not empirical recalibration. All other H41-A construction and exclusion settings remain fixed.

## Validation: previous-session next step first

1. Preserve the failed H41-A/H41-E strict 20 km audit unchanged. Evaluate H41-I on its exact four folds as a regression/negative-control audit, not a fresh independent model-selection test. Remove whole source IDs intersecting the test polygons plus 300 m; do not invent clipping endpoints.
2. Add a **conditional short-strand transfer-habitat audit**, the missing next step from the previous session. Eligibility is determined once before scores: a source has total included part length ≤10 km and its length-weighted centroid is within 5 km of both an eligible NW and an eligible N/NNE source other than itself. Remove whole target source IDs grouped by centroid into four geographic quadrants of the grid. Donor/receiver context from non-target sources remains visible, including in test quadrants. This is deliberately **conditional missing-strand reconstruction**, NOT blind geographic extrapolation.
3. Rebuild training catalogue by rasterizing retained vectors, never by subtracting target pixels from the full raster. All family distances, nearest tangents, endpoint connectivity and exclusions are recomputed from retained geometry. No removed-source geometry may enter model inputs. Truth is rasterized withheld complete sources. Score the corresponding 300 m-eroded quadrant within the footprint and >200 m from retained catalogue. Truth outside the evaluated region is not scored. Record target count, number of sources, test truth pixels, support and confidence mass.
4. Compare H41-I to freshly rebuilt H41-A, a geometry-only ablation, catalogue-density and topography-only controls at matched H41-I prediction mass per fold (scale with clipping using bisection). No historical raster is a model input or a clean comparator. Report all four folds even if empty; mark no-truth folds unevaluable. Report mean and pooled sufficient-statistic DTI; no tuning or cross-validation confidence interval from four correlated folds.
5. This audit's target selection uses known geometry and favors short strands in transfer habitat. That selection bias, visible context inside quadrants, vector/raster label mismatch, imperfect faults, and reuse of the same region prevent interpreting it as an independent hidden-fault benchmark. Nearby duplicate traces can remain; report withheld truth within 300 m of training geometry and exclude the 200 m zone from scoring. Never call it a leakage-free organizer validation.
6. The weekly slot stays closed unless the candidate beats H41-A AND both non-vacuous controls in all evaluable conditional folds, all four folds have truth and positive prediction support, passes concentration/format/novelty checks, **and** beats a clean historical-best OOF benchmark. That last benchmark is absent, so no slot may be spent regardless of this experiment's result.

## Export and novelty gate

Build H41-I from full raw geometry and band 12 after validation, even if unsuccessful, as a clearly labeled research-only GeoTIFF. Require more junction confidence than both NW-only and N/NNE-only density controls, >50% of the highest 5% positive confidence cells in the pre-existing corridor-junction definition, and zero prediction ≤200 m from catalogue. Compare actual arrays to every checked-in TIFF and the pinned H33 educational raster **after construction**; different file names or tags alone do not establish novelty. Compare floats in the valid footprint and nonzero support; report overlap. Global uniqueness against unavailable private files cannot be proven.

Export single-band float32, exact template grid/CRS, all numerical cells finite [0,1], outside values zero with internal mask; reopen and validate. Save input/code hashes, note, receipt, and result even if gate fails. No DrivenData requests or submission automation.
